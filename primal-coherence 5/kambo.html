Plant Medicine

Kambo

Kambo is where spirit and science meet. It is ancient medicine with a biochemistry so sophisticated that modern science is still working to fully understand it. It is not comfortable, and it does not ask your permission. But what it offers on the other side — physical renewal, emotional liberation, and a quality of clarity that most people have never felt — is why it holds a special place at the heart of this practice.

What Kambo is

Kambo is the secretion of the Phyllomedusa bicolor — the giant leaf frog of the Amazon rainforest, known to indigenous peoples as the frog of light. It has been used by tribes including the Matsés, Katukina, and Yawanawá for centuries — not as a drug, but as a sacred tool for physical purification, energetic cleansing, and the strengthening of body, mind, and spirit. Traditionally used to prepare warriors before hunting and to clear what these traditions call panema — the accumulated heaviness of stagnant energy, illness, and misfortune — Kambo's migration to the West has brought it into a new context without diminishing its power.

The secretion is collected ethically from the living frog, which is never harmed. The frog is gently held, the secretion carefully scraped onto wooden sticks, and the frog released back into the forest. This is considered sacred practice — the frog is treated as an ally, not a resource. Kambo is not psychoactive. It does not produce hallucinations or alter perception. What it produces is a full-body purge — and what follows that purge is the reason people seek it out again and again.

The science — natural peptide therapy

In 1986, Italian pharmacologist Vittorio Erspamer described Kambo as containing "a fantastic chemical cocktail with potential medical applications unequalled by any other amphibian." That description has only become more accurate with time. What makes Kambo extraordinary is not any single compound but the synergy of its complete peptide profile. These are short-chain amino acids — the same building blocks the body uses to regulate nearly every physiological process. The body recognizes Kambo's peptides as beneficial at a cellular level, allowing them to cross the blood-brain barrier and act directly on the nervous system, lymphatic system, organs, and cells. Dozens of pharmaceutical patents have been filed on its isolated peptides. There is no synthetic substitute. The only way to receive this complete, naturally balanced cocktail is through the real secretion.

Dermorphin & Deltorphin
Natural opioid peptides with pain-relieving properties significantly more potent than morphine. They bind to opioid receptors without the dependency risk of synthetic opioids.
Phyllocaerulein
Stimulates the adrenal cortex and pituitary gland, supports digestion, and plays a role in the deep detoxification Kambo initiates throughout the digestive tract.
Phyllomedusin
A powerful vasodilator that excites neurons, contracts smooth muscles, and stimulates one of the deepest gastrointestinal cleanses the body can undergo.
Phyllokinin
Targets bradykinin receptors to relax arterial smooth muscle, supporting healthy circulation and blood pressure.
Adenoregulin
Works with adenosine receptors to support cellular health, immune function, and mood regulation. Laboratory research has demonstrated its effectiveness against drug-resistant pathogens.
Dermaseptin
A broad-spectrum antimicrobial peptide with demonstrated activity against bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and parasites. Among the most extensively researched of Kambo's compounds.
Sauvagine
Affects the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis — the body's central stress response system — and plays a key role in the adrenal reset many people experience after ceremony.
Caerulein
Stimulates smooth muscle in the digestive tract and gallbladder, supporting liver and digestive detoxification. Also has analgesic and hypotensive properties.
Research note

Laboratory research has demonstrated Kambo peptides to be effective against drug-resistant strains of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and viruses — opening pathways for new classes of antimicrobial treatment. Research into Kambo's anti-tumor properties is ongoing at multiple institutions.

Physical healing

Kambo is one of the strongest natural anti-inflammatory, antibiotic, antimicrobial, and analgesic substances found anywhere on earth. When the peptides enter the lymphatic system through the gates, they initiate a full-system sweep — clearing the liver and intestines of accumulated toxins, stimulating the immune system at a cellular level, reducing systemic inflammation, and flushing the body of pathogens and accumulated waste that conventional detox approaches cannot reach.

People seek Kambo for a wide range of physical conditions: chronic pain and inflammation, chronic fatigue and burnout, autoimmune disorders, Lyme disease, recurring infections, hormone imbalances, menstrual disorders, digestive dysfunction, thyroid issues, fertility challenges, and as a powerful periodic reset for people in good health who want to clear the system before it becomes heavy. Immediate post-ceremony effects consistently include enhanced energy, improved circulation, reduced pain, and a physical lightness that people describe as feeling clean from the inside out.

Emotional healing

The peptides in Kambo act directly on the brain's neurotransmitter systems — stimulating the release of endorphins and mood-regulating compounds, balancing the HPA axis that governs the body's stress response, and resetting neural pathways dysregulated by trauma and chronic stress.

What is most significant is how Kambo releases emotional material. Unlike talk therapy, which asks the conscious mind to revisit and process what happened, Kambo works at the physiological level — the body purges what it is ready to release without the mind needing to narrate or explain it. Emotions that have been compressed for years surface and move through. Patterns that have persisted despite years of inner work shift. The purge is not only physical. It is also one of the most thorough forms of emotional release available.

Kambo releases trauma without requiring you to relive it. The body knows what it is ready to let go of. Your job is simply to surrender to the process and trust that what is leaving needed to leave.

Spiritual healing

Indigenous traditions describe Kambo as clearing panema — the energetic heaviness that dims the light, dulls intuition, brings bad luck, and keeps people cycling through the same stagnant experiences. This is not separate from the physical and emotional work. It is the same process viewed from a different level. Both are being cleared simultaneously.

What many people experience in the days following Kambo is a quality of presence, openness, and connection that feels like returning to something essential — something they had forgotten was there. A sense of alignment. Of moving through life with less resistance. Of things falling into place that had been stuck for years.

What happens in a ceremony

Every session at Primal Coherence begins with a thorough intake consultation — reviewing your health history, medications, intentions, and contraindications. This is required, not optional. The ceremony space is prepared and held as sacred container.

You arrive having fasted for 8–12 hours. Small, superficial burns called gates are made on the skin — typically on the arm or leg — affecting only the top layer of skin, creating pathways through which the medicine enters the lymphatic system. You then drink 1.5 to 2 liters of water. The dried secretion is rehydrated and applied to the gates. We always begin with a test point to assess your body's response before proceeding.

The medicine moves immediately. Warmth and flushing in the face and body, accelerated heart rate, pressure in the head. Then the purge begins — vomiting is the most common release, along with sweating, emotional release, sound, and sometimes tears. The most intense phase typically lasts 10 to 20 minutes. Once complete, the medicine is removed from the gates. The gates are dressed with Sangre de Grado — dragon's blood tree sap — which seals the wounds, prevents infection, and supports healing. You rest. You are held. When you are ready, you break your fast with light, nourishing food. The total ceremony including preparation, experience, and integration time is typically 2 to 4 hours.

Most people feel the full benefits of Kambo within 24 to 72 hours — a physical lightness, enhanced energy and mental clarity, emotional openness, and a quality of presence that can last for weeks.

Preparation
  • Fast for 8–12 hours before your session — no food, juice, or supplements
  • Drink your normal daily water intake the day before — do not overhydrate
  • No alcohol or recreational drugs for at least 48–72 hours prior
  • Hold SSRIs or serotonin-affecting medications on the day of your session — consult your prescribing physician before doing so
  • Eat light, clean food in the days leading up to your ceremony
  • No saunas, sweat lodges, or hot tubs the day before or after
  • No colonics or enemas in the week prior
  • Arrive with an intention — not a requirement, but a powerful amplifier
After your ceremony

Rest. Eat a light nourishing meal. Hydrate. Be gentle with yourself. Avoid alcohol, drugs, and intense exertion for at least 24 hours. The days following can bring emotional sensitivity as the integration process continues — this is the work still unfolding. Time in nature, journaling, and quiet reflection deepen what the medicine initiated. The gates scab naturally within a day and fade over weeks to months. Many people wear their marks with quiet pride.

Contraindications — please read carefully
  • Serious heart conditions, heart disease, or heart medication
  • Low or high blood pressure, or blood pressure medication
  • History of stroke, brain hemorrhage, or aneurysm
  • Active serious psychiatric conditions — bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, psychosis
  • Pregnancy
  • Addison's disease or Cushing's disease
  • Active liver or kidney disease
  • Undergoing chemotherapy or radiotherapy, or within 4 weeks of completing it
  • Active serious immune disorders
  • Lack of capacity to give full informed consent

A detailed intake form is completed before every session. Please be fully honest — your safety depends on it. If you are unsure whether Kambo is appropriate for your specific health situation, consult your physician before booking.
