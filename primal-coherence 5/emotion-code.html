Remote sessions that work with your subconscious to find and permanently release the trapped emotions, energetic imbalances, and hidden stressors that are quietly running the show — showing up as physical pain, emotional patterns, relationship blocks, or the persistent sense that something is holding you back. The premise — energy as the foundation of health

Every emotion is a form of energy. When it's fully felt and expressed, it moves through and releases. But when an emotion arrives in a moment when you can't safely process it — when it's too overwhelming, too dangerous, or simply interrupted — it doesn't disappear. It gets trapped. It lodges as a discrete energetic imprint somewhere in the body, quietly distorting the field around it, influencing physical tissue, organ function, emotional patterns, and behavior. Sometimes for decades.

Dr. Bradley Nelson — a holistic physician with over two decades of clinical practice — identified this pattern across thousands of patients. The chronic pain, the emotional cycling, the relationship struggles, the blocks to success: so much of it traced back not to a structural problem or a chemical imbalance, but to trapped emotional energy the body had never been able to release. The Emotion Code and Body Code are his systematic methods for identifying and clearing exactly that.

Muscle testing — communicating with the subconscious

The subconscious mind is the body's operating system — storing every experience, regulating every physiological process, and holding precise knowledge of what is out of balance. Most healing approaches work almost entirely with the conscious mind, which has access to a fraction of the body's total intelligence. Muscle testing — applied kinesiology — bypasses that limitation entirely.

It works through a well-documented principle: the body's electrical system responds differently to stimuli that are true versus false, harmonious versus dissonant. When a true statement is made, the system remains strong. When a false or incongruent input is introduced, the electrical system briefly short-circuits and muscle response drops. The body is answering yes or no questions through its own physiology — a direct line to the subconscious that no amount of conscious analysis can replicate. In remote sessions, this is done through proxy muscle testing, which is equally effective because the work operates at the level of energy and information rather than physical proximity.

The Emotion Code

Using muscle testing and a chart of 60 emotions organized by the organ systems they correspond to, the Emotion Code identifies which specific emotions are trapped in your body, where they are, when they became trapped, and whether they are your own, absorbed from someone else, or inherited across generations. Once identified, each emotion is released by running a magnet along the Governing Meridian — the master energy channel running from the forehead over the crown and down the spine. The release is permanent. That emotion, once cleared, does not return.

Trapped emotions can be your own, absorbed from others — particularly in childhood — or inherited through family lines, encoded in the energetic blueprint you received before birth. Epigenetic research supports the idea that the effects of significant emotional and traumatic events can be transmitted biologically across generations. The Emotion Code works with all of these as part of its standard protocol.

The Heart-Wall

When the subconscious perceives the heart is in danger of being broken — through grief, betrayal, abandonment, or repeated heartbreak — it does something protective and ultimately costly. It takes trapped emotional energies and builds a wall around the heart with them. Layer by layer. The Heart-Wall blocks pain in the short term. In the long term it blocks everything: love, joy, connection, intuition, the sense of being fully alive in your own life.

You may feel emotionally flat even around people you care about. Like there's a glass wall between you and real intimacy. Like love can't quite land. The Heart-Wall acts as a filter, dulling both pain and joy simultaneously. In Dr. Nelson's clinical experience, the Heart-Wall is among the most common findings. Clearing it — layer by layer across several sessions — is consistently described as one of the most significant shifts people experience. The heart, when unblocked, changes everything.

Having a Heart-Wall is like living in a bomb shelter. It's useful while there's danger. But if you keep it forever, you're not living — you're surviving.

The Body Code — the full map

The Body Code expands the Emotion Code into a comprehensive system for identifying imbalances across every dimension of health. The subconscious navigates six categories, finding root causes that often combine multiple factors — a trapped emotion, a circuit disruption, a nutritional deficiency — all contributing simultaneously to a single presenting issue.

Energies
Trapped emotions, absorbed and inherited emotions, post-traumatic energies, and other negative energetic influences.
Circuits & Systems
Imbalances in organs, glands, muscles, meridians, and chakras — disruptions in the energetic connections between body systems.
Toxins
Heavy metals, chemicals, environmental toxins, EMF radiation, and free radicals in physical and energetic form.
Pathogens
Fungal, bacterial, viral, mold, or parasitic activity disrupting the system — physically present or energetically active.
Misalignments
Structural imbalances in bones, nerves, and connective tissue — often microscopic, often rooted in associated emotional imbalances.
Nutrition & Lifestyle
Deficiencies, sleep disruption, dehydration, and specific nutrients or herbs the body is asking for in order to heal.

What happens in a session

Sessions are done remotely — by phone or video. You don't need to be in a specific place or position. You don't need to know what is wrong, recall specific memories, or have conscious access to what is being released. The subconscious holds all of it. It surfaces only what is ready to be released in that session, in the order most appropriate for your system. The practitioner communicates the findings as the session unfolds, and you receive a written summary afterward.

In the 24 to 72 hours following a session, it's common to notice mild fatigue, emotional surfacing, or vivid dreams — the body integrating what has been cleared. Drinking water and resting supports this process. Most people notice shifts quietly: old reactions that no longer fire, patterns that loosen, a physical symptom that simply isn't there anymore.
